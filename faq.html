The season

What is Imitatio?

A school of drawing and painting at the Studio of Mark Balma, and a restorative retreat for painters, writers and other creatives: a quiet retreat with community, in the foothills of Assisi, Italy.

What are the formats?

La Stagione: the full six-week season, the flagship. L’Intensivo: three weeks, joining the first or second half of a season, for those who can’t step away for six.

When are the seasons?

Spring and Autumn 2027. Exact dates: to be announced

Who teaches?

The maestro Mark Balma, one of the few painters in the world still working in true buon fresco.

What will I study?

Drawing (pencil, charcoal, silverpoint, red and black chalk, ink), egg tempera, and oil in the 15th-century manner of the Flemish masters and Leonardo. Accomplished students work with Mark on buon fresco murals.

La casa · Life at the house

Where will I stay?

At the house, an Italian villa in the fields and foothills below Assisi, the land St. Francis walked. Rooms: to be announced

What about meals?

La Tavola: a chef’s table in the vaulted refectory, and outdoor dining. Whether meals are included: to be announced

Is there a chapel?

Yes. The old stable is becoming the chapel: benches made from the old wooden mangers, and a few of Mark’s frescoes. Mass and prayer schedule: to be announced

Will there be quiet time?

Plenty of quiet time to pray, meditate and rest.

Is there community?

Yes. The allievi share one table, and the season is lived in community.

Will there be pilgrimages?

The allievi come as pilgrims, and the land of saints and masters around Assisi invites pilgrimage. Which places, how often and any cost: to be announced
