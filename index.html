Il Maestro

Learn from the Master

Art is learned by seeing. Mastery is learned by doing.

MARK BALMA trained in Florence under Pietro Annigoni and is one of the few painters in the world still working in true buon fresco. He has painted portraits from sittings with four US Presidents and three British Prime Ministers. Now, in his studio below Assisi, he opens the bottega to a new generation.

La Villa

Life at the Villa

Create. Rest. Pray. Belong.

Days are spent at the easel in the bottega. Meals are shared at one table in the vaulted refectory. The chapel, La Cappella, is kept for prayer. Beyond the walls, the road winds up to Assisi.
