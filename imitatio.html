The Season

The first season opens in 2027 · Spring and Autumn

The focus of the school is finding the sacred and spiritual in art through the study of God's creation. For a season, allievi (students) live at the villa in the foothills of Assisi, working in the bottega (workshop) alongside the maestro.

In Mark's words: “It will be a restorative place for painters, writers etc. a quiet retreat with community.”

“My house was on the road St. Francis would walk to Gubbio.”Mark Balma, of his earlier home near Assisi
“Art imitates nature as well as it can, as a pupil follows his master; thus it is sort of a grandchild of God.”Dante Alighieri

What you will study

Drawing

Students will learn the discipline of drawing, hand-eye coordination, and the observation of light and shadow while incorporating methods of the Old Masters: pencil, charcoal, silverpoint, red/black chalk and ink.

Painting

Mark Balma instructs students in the use of egg tempera and oil painting as practiced in 15th-century Europe by the Flemish Masters and Leonardo da Vinci. Accomplished students will also learn the art of buon fresco, working directly with Mark Balma on fresco murals.

The tools

  • Pencil
  • Charcoal
  • Silverpoint
  • Red and black chalk
  • Ink
  • Egg tempera
  • Oil
  • Buon fresco: lime plaster and pigment

Pilgrimages

In Assisi the art and the saints are one story. St. Francis lies in the Basilica that Cimabue and Giotto filled with frescoes; St. Clare of Assisi followed him in the same hills. Students come as pilgrims, not tourists: to Norcia, Cascia, Foligno, Perugia and Terni.

Saints of Umbria

  • St. Francis and St. Clare of Assisi
  • St. Benedict and St. ScholasticaTwins, born in Norcia.
  • St. Carlo AcutisCanonized in 2025, buried in Assisi.
  • St. Gabriel of Our Lady of SorrowsBorn in Assisi.
  • St. Rita of Cascia
  • St. Angela of Foligno
  • St. ValentineBishop of Terni.

The masters

  • Cimabue and GiottoFrescoes in the Basilica of St. Francis, Assisi.
  • PeruginoWho taught the young Raphael in Perugia.
