Il Maestro

Mark Balma

Trained at Richard Lack's atelier and invited to Florence at sixteen by Pietro Annigoni, Mark Balma has spent his life on fresco, portraiture and sacred painting, from the Cathedral of St. Paul to a chapel in Assisi dedicated to Clare.

Portraits of four US Presidents and three British Prime Ministers for Presidential Libraries.
