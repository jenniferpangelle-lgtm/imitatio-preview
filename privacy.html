Privacy Policy

This site belongs to the Studio of Mark Balma, Imitatio, in the foothills of Assisi, Italy. This page explains what happens to the information you send us.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send through the application form or the contact form: for example your name, email address, phone number, country, your answers to the application questions and your message.

How the forms work

Both forms are processed by Formspree, a form service. Your submission is passed by email to the studio, where it is read and screened.

How we use it

We use what you send only to review your application or to reply to your message. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Your faith

The application question about your faith or calling is optional. What you share about your faith is sensitive personal data, so we only process it with your explicit consent, given with the checkbox under that question. You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to the studio.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information you sent us, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Write to us through the contact page.

On this site

Our fonts are served from this site, not from a third-party font service. Videos load from YouTube’s privacy-enhanced service only when you press play.
